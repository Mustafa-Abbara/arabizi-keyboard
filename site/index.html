Type Arabizi. Get Arabic.

Harfi turns Lebanese Arabizi into Arabic as you type, in every app on your Mac. Privately: nothing you type ever leaves it.

Install for Mac

Free · Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later · Windows later

Why Harfi

It speaks Lebanese

Trained on how people really text: ma bade 2rou7 becomes ما بدي روح, and your English stays English.

Private by design

The conversion runs on your Mac with no internet access. No account, no tracking, no cloud.

Fix a word in a tap

See the Arabic as you type, press ↑ to fix a word before sending, and Harfi remembers your spelling.

Install

Open Terminal (press ⌘ Space, type Terminal), paste this line and press Enter:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mustafa-Abbara/arabizi-keyboard/main/scripts/install_harfi.sh | bash
  1. It installs Harfi and downloads its model (808 MB). If your connection drops, it carries on where it stopped.
  2. Turn it on once: System Settings → Keyboard → Input Sources → Edit… → + → Arabic → Harfi.
  3. Switch to it with Ctrl Space and type.

Why Terminal? Harfi is free and not signed with a paid Apple Developer ID, so a normal download would be blocked; the install line sets it up on your own Mac instead. Full guide

The keys to know

Enter
Your text becomes Arabic. Enter again sends.
↑
Fix a word before converting: ← → choose the word, a number picks its spelling.
⌥ Tab
Fix a word right after converting.
⌘ Z
Your Latin text back.
